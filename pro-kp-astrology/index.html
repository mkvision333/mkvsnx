FAQ

Common questions.

Is Pro KP Astrology intended for casual users or professionals?

It is suitable for both, but the feature set is especially helpful for users who regularly work with charts, consultation preparation, or structured astrology references.

Does the app guarantee outcomes or predictions?

No. The app provides interpretive and informational tools. It does not guarantee results or outcomes in personal, legal, financial, or professional matters.

Why might storage permission be requested?

Storage-related access may be needed for saved charts, exported content, or user-selected files depending on the feature being used.
