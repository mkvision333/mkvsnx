Privacy Center

Privacy information organized by product.

MKVSNX publishes a dedicated Privacy Policy for every application. This approach helps users, store reviewers, institutions, and partners understand the policy information relevant to each product rather than relying on a single generic statement.

Why separate policies matter

Different applications may involve different categories of information. A construction app may work with site photos and expense records. A school app may work with attendance and parent communication. A media app may handle photos and videos. Separate policies improve clarity and help users understand what is relevant to their use case.

How to use this page

Select the application you are using to open its dedicated Privacy Policy. Each policy is intended to describe the data practices relevant to that application and should be updated whenever the app's permissions, SDKs, or data handling change. If you have questions about data access, deletion, permissions, or third-party services, please review the relevant policy first and then contact mkvision333@gmail.com if further clarification is needed.
